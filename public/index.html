Today

Today list

Drag ⠿ to reorder, or on a phone press and hold a task, then drag. Drop a task on a day at the top to move it to the end of that day. Drag the time pill left or right to change how long, or tap it to choose. Tap the padlock to hold a task at its time while the rest flow round it, or tap a task’s time to put it at an exact time. Appointments come from Google Calendar and tasks are planned around them. Temp tasks stay on their day and never go to Todoist.

Next actions

Drag a task into the day’s list (press and hold on a phone), or press +, to give it a time on the day you have chosen. Drop it on a day at the top to put it on that day instead. Drag one back here to take its time off. Tick a next action and Docket asks what comes after it.
